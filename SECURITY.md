# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Report it privately: on GitHub, open the **Security** tab of `raess-dev/astro-kit` and choose
**Report a vulnerability**. Do not open a public issue, discussion or pull request.

Include the kit version, what an attacker can do, and the smallest steps or site that shows it. You will get an
answer within seven days. A fix ships as a new kit version, and the advisory is published once sites can upgrade.

## Scope

- The `@raess/astro-kit` package: the integration, its checks, the components, the contact endpoint, the CLI and
  the templates it writes, including the site workflow.
- Not in scope: a site's own content, its Cloudflare or GitHub settings, and the services the kit relies on. Report
  those to the site owner or the vendor.

## Supported versions

Only the latest published version gets fixes.
