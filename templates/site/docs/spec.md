# The brief

The business brief of the site. Agents read it before they write a page. The owner gives every fact and every legal
text; an agent never invents one. Brand and design live in `docs/design.md`.

## Audience

Who reads the site, and what each group of readers wants from it.

## Locales

The default locale, every other locale and its coverage, and the words of the glossary (`glossary.json`).

## Pages

One entry for each page: its intent, its audience, its message and its call to action.

## Facts

The facts live in `site.json` and `schedule.json`: the name, the addresses, the numbers, the prices and the opening
hours. A page reads them from there and never types them.

## Legal sources

Where the texts of the legal note and of the privacy pages come from, and who approved them.

## Open questions

Each question for the owner, until it has an answer.
